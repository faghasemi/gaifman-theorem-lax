This submission formalizes Gaifman's normal-form theorem for first-order
logic over finite relational vocabularies. It develops the syntax and
semantics of relational structures and first-order formulas, the associated
Gaifman graph and its metric neighborhoods, local formulas, and uniform basic
local sentences.

The main result states that every first-order formula is equivalent to a
Boolean combination of formulas local around its free variables and vocabulary
constants, and sentences asserting the existence of pairwise distant witnesses
satisfying one fixed local formula. In the sentence case every constituent is
closed by construction. Each constituent records its own radius, and all such
radii satisfy the theorem's uniform exponential bound. The formalization
follows Gaifman's original theorem and the formulation in Libkin's *Elements
of Finite Model Theory*.
